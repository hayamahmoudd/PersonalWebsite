01

Introduction

Welcome to my Summer 2026 co-op report about my experience as a Web Applications Developer Co-op at Kenna.

In mid December 2025, I began searching for my first co-op opportunity and came across Kenna's Web Applications Developer position. I was immediately drawn to the company's unique intersection of agriculture and software.

After speaking with former co-op students and current developers, I became even more confident that Kenna would be a great place to learn. They described a welcoming environment, friendly coworkers, and many opportunities to connect through social events.

My time as a "Kennadian" gave me the opportunity to meet amazing people, strengthen my technical and communication skills, and gain valuable experience working as part of a professional software development team.

02

Information About Kenna

About Kenna

Kenna is a Mississauga-based marketing solutions company that helps organizations create better and more connected experiences for their customers. Its work brings together strategy, data, technology, design, and marketing to support clients throughout the customer journey. What interested me most about Kenna was how closely its creative and technical teams work together. The company does more than build websites or marketing campaigns. It develops customized platforms, internal systems, data solutions, and digital experiences based on each client's needs.

One thing that stood out to me was Kenna's people-first environment. The company places a strong emphasis on Customer First Thinking, and I saw that same mindset reflected in the way co-ops and full-time employees are supported.

Location and Work Environment

Kenna's office is located at 90 Burnhamthorpe Road West in downtown Mississauga, just a five minute walk from Square One. The area is lively and convenient, with plenty of restaurants, public transit options, and places nearby to spend more of my paycheque than I probably should.

I followed a hybrid schedule, working in the office on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays and remotely on Mondays and Fridays. The in-office days made it easier to collaborate, ask questions, and stay connected with the team, while the remote days gave me more uninterrupted time to focus on development work.

We will help you learn the concepts, technologies, frameworks, and tools for a career in web development by providing hands-on training and professional development tailored to students' needs and capabilities. Work with industry veterans and other students to build professional relationships, contacts and friendships for your future career. Don't believe us? Ask all the developers we've hired that were former co-op students.

05

Conclusions

In Summer 2026, I was given an amazing opportunity to begin my career at a company I truly wanted to be part of. After speaking with former co-op students and current Kenna employees, I had a strong feeling that Kenna would be the right place for me, and looking back on the past four months, I am grateful that I trusted that feeling.

Throughout the term, I grew not only as a developer, but also as a person. I strengthened my technical skills, became more confident in my work, and developed a stronger mindset when facing challenges. Working with people outside the development team, including the accounts, data, and QA teams, also improved my communication skills and helped me understand how software is built and supported in a real industry environment.

One of the best parts of the experience was meeting more than 20 other co-op students, many of whom were fellow Gryphons. I built friendships that I hope will continue through school and beyond this work term. It was also extremely rewarding to see my work used by more than 20,000 users across Canada and the United States. Knowing that something I contributed to had a real impact made me even more excited to continue pursuing a career in software development.

Most importantly, this work term gave me the confidence to truly see myself as a developer. I am leaving Kenna with stronger skills, better work habits, meaningful connections, a deeper appreciation for the people and teamwork behind great software, and, of course, the title of being a Kennadian!
